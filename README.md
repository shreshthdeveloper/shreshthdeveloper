## About

I build distributed backend systems that carry real production volume, and I currently lead a team of six engineers across five microservices at **Riktam Technologies**.

Most of my work sits in supply-chain infrastructure — **5,000 to 25,000 orders per day** on Python, Kafka and MongoDB. The other half is production GenAI: retrieval pipelines, agent orchestration, and the evaluation and cost tooling that keeps them viable at volume.

**Currently working on**

- Backend architecture for **Growsari**, a B2B distribution platform
- **SPT**, a 120-day replenishment planning engine that surfaces stockouts before they occur
- LangGraph agents with RAG, tool calling, eval harnesses and token-cost optimization
- Event-driven design and distributed systems at scale

**Open to conversations about** system design, event-driven architecture, and agent systems that survive contact with production.
